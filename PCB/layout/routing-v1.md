# pa40-classe rev 7: routing v1

2026-11-19, KiCad 10.0.8. Generated by `layout/route.py`, which loads
`old/pre-routing/pa40-classe.after-L2swap.kicad_pcb` (placement v1 plus the L2 pin swap, no copper), adds every

and re-run it; it is idempotent. Backups of the pre-routing board, schematic, project, gen.py and symbol library
are in `old/pre-routing/`.

## Pin swap (schematic + PCB)
- **L2 swapped** (kept): `gen.py` places L2 with `mir='y'`; the schematic instance has `(mirror y)` added in place
  (no gen.py re-run, so all symbol UUIDs and footprint links are unchanged). L2 pad 2 is now the C2 end
  (Net-(C2-Pad2), the pad next to C2) and pad 1 is `/out_50r`. C2-to-L2 run is 13.4 mm (was about 22 mm).
- **L1 swap reverted.** It was applied first, then undone: a pre-swap check showed the drain and +12V_PA runs did
  not cross on the original pinning, and the swap would have lengthened the drain run (about 43 V peak, high
  dv/dt) from about 11 mm to 20-22 mm. L1 is back to the original numbering: pad 2 (top, 157.89/144.99) = `/drain`,
  pad 1 (bottom, 157.89/152.61) = `/+12V_PA`. gen.py and the schematic are byte-identical to the backup for L1.
- Netlist diff before/after: only L2's two pin numbers change.

## Rules (JLCPCB standard 2-layer, 1.6 mm, 1 oz)
- Board minimums: 0.127 mm clearance and track, 0.3 mm drill, 0.5 mm via, 0.13 mm annular ring, 0.5 mm
  hole-to-hole, 0.25 mm hole-to-copper, 0.5 mm copper-to-edge. Netclass Default: 0.2 mm clearance.
- All vias 0.6/0.3 mm.
- `pa40-classe.kicad_dru`: one rule, 0.15 mm clearance for items touching Q1 only. The EPC2036 land pattern
  has 0.15 mm pad gaps (0.45 mm pitch, 0.3 mm pads). JLCPCB standard 2-layer minimum spacing is 0.127 mm (5 mil),
  so 0.15 mm is inside capability (no advanced-process charge). The gate track into the gate bump is 0.25 mm wide
  and clears the source bump by 0.18 mm; the drain track (0.25 mm) leaves away from the gate bump.

## Ground
- Solid GND pour on B.Cu (the only B.Cu tracks are three short hops, below) and GND pour on F.Cu.
  Zone clearance 0.3 mm, min width 0.25 mm. SMD pads connect solid; through-hole pads use 0.5 mm thermal spokes
  (hand soldering).
- 421 GND vias: 4 mm grid board-wide, 3 mm grid over the gate driver / Q1 / tank / LPF / output, the XO and the
  buck, a fence along the RF output run, plus placed vias at the Q1 source (3), U1 GND, the TPS62175 exposed pad
  (2 via-in-pad), and C19's ground pad. Each stitching via is checked by the script to clear all non-GND copper by
  0.35 mm, all drills by 0.6 mm, SMD courtyards and copper text.
- H1-H4 are unchanged (no net), so their pad rings are not grounded.

## RF output line
- The brief asked for about 2.9 mm microstrip. That is right for plain microstrip (Hammerstad-Jensen, h = 1.51 mm,
  er 4.5, 35 um: 2.9 mm -> 48.6 ohm, 50 ohm at 2.77 mm). With a top GND pour beside it the line becomes coplanar,
  and 2.9 mm with the normal 0.3 mm pour gap would be about 38 ohm.
- So L3 -> C19 -> J2 uses **2.4 mm track with a 1.0 mm gap to the F.Cu pour** over the B.Cu plane: about 51 ohm
  (CPWG, conformal mapping, er 4.5; 50.7 ohm at er 4.5, 53 at er 4.3 with 2.2 mm). The gap is a F.Cu "no pour"
  rule area named `rf_out CPWG gap`, made by inflating the 2.4 mm tracks by 1.0 mm.
- At 7 MHz the run (about 12 mm) is about 0.001 wavelength, so impedance is not critical. It mainly keeps the
  shunt C at J2 low and the return path tight.

## Crossings (bottom-layer hops)
All are outside the RF section. Total B.Cu signal copper is 11.6 mm.
1. +12V under +5V and the XO output: B.Cu 4.8 mm at x = 129.5, between y = 131.0 and 135.8.
2. +12V from JP1 pad 2 to the trunk: B.Cu 2.9 mm (JP1 is boxed in by the shunt pads and the Kelvin sense trace).
3. Net-(Q2-G): TP15 to R3 (3.9 mm). The +12V feed to R2/Q2 source is fenced in by the Q2 gate net on one side and
   +12V_PA on the other, so one of the two has to hop. The low-current gate net hops.

## Loops
- **Gate loop:** U1 pin 4 -> R1 -> Q1 gate is about 4.5 mm (0.4 mm track, 0.25 mm into the bump). The return goes Q1 source -> 3 vias
  -> plane -> U1 GND via (U1 pin 3 to its via is 1.6 mm).
- **Power loop:** Q1 drain -> C1 (0.25 + 0.8 mm, about 2 mm) -> C1 GND -> 0.6 mm track back to the Q1 source bumps
  (about 2.5 x 2 mm loop). C14/C15 GND ends tie to the same source track; their +12V_PA ends are on the 1.0 mm feed.
- **Buck:** follows the TI TPS62175 layout example: Cin (C21) on VIN/PGND with a direct PGND track, Cout (C22)
  ground tied straight to PGND and the EP, SW node short (U2 pin 9 straight into L4), FB divider R13/R14 next
  to the FB pin, and 2 vias in the EP.

## Items for Jason to double-check
1. **+12V_PA runs under L1's core.** The 1.0 mm feed from C15 to L1 pad 1 goes diagonally under the flat FT50-61
   toroid. It is solder-masked and the core is ferrite, so this is fine electrically, but it is mechanical
   contact with the winding. The alternative is a run beside the drain trace for about 10 mm, which is worse for
   coupling.
2. **SLEEP/FB-top tie loops around L4.** U2's SLEEP pin (8) and VOS pin (10) are on opposite sides of SW (pin 9),
   so the 0.25 mm SLEEP/R13 line runs around the left and bottom of L4 to L4 pad 2 (Vout). This is DC with very
   low current, but there is no top pour inside that loop. A B.Cu hop under the buck would cut the plane there
   instead.
3. **Via-in-pad on the TPS62175 EP** (2 x 0.3 mm). These help heat and allow soldering the pad from the back,
   but they can wick solder during hot-air rework. Ask JLCPCB for tented or plugged vias if wanted.
4. **2.4 mm RF track covers C19 pad 1 fully.** Solder will spread along the track, so expect to use more solder or
   flux there.
5. Check that the 0.15 mm Q1 land-pattern rule is acceptable on the order (JLCPCB minimum is 0.127 mm).
6. H1-H4 are not grounded (no net). Add H1-H4 to the schematic on GND if you want chassis ground through the
   standoffs.
7. Rev 7 marking (F.Cu text) is unchanged. The pour keeps 0.3 mm around it.
